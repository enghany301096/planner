In this privacy notice, we seek to explain to you in the clearest way possible what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to it.

1What Information Do We Collect?

Personal information you disclose to us

We do not collect any personal data directly. The App is designed to store your data (tasks, expenses, and settings) locally on your device using SQLite databases and SharedPreferences. This means your personal and financial data never leaves your device unless you choose to back it up or share it.

Information collected through third-party services

While we do not collect your personal data, the App may use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. The third-party service providers used by the App include:

  • Firebase / Google Analytics: To help us understand how the App is being used and to identify bugs or crashes.
  • Speech To Text: Voice recognition data is processed by your device's native speech recognition services (e.g., Google Speech Services on Android, Apple Speech on iOS) according to their respective privacy policies.

2How Do We Use Your Information?

Since your data is stored locally, we do not have access to your tasks or financial records.

Any diagnostic data collected via third-party services (like Firebase) is used solely to:

  • Monitor and analyze usage and trends to improve your experience with the App.
  • Troubleshoot software bugs and operational problems.

3Will Your Information Be Shared With Anyone?

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any of your information with third parties for their promotional purposes.

4How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

Your local data is kept for as long as you have the App installed on your device. You can delete all your data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's data from your device settings.

5How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

We rely on the built-in security features of your mobile operating system (iOS or Android). Additionally, the App may offer biometric authentication (Face ID/Touch ID) to restrict physical access to your financial data within the App. However, please remember that we cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet (e.g., via third-party integrations).

6Do We Make Updates To This Notice?

We may update this privacy notice from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Last updated" date, and the updated version will be effective as soon as it is accessible. We encourage you to review this privacy notice frequently to be informed of how we are protecting your information.
